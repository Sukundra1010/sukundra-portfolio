About Me

An overview of my academic background, technical expertise, and professional aspirations in software development and emerging technologies.

Focus Areas: Python Development, Web Development with Django, AI and Machine Learning Fundamentals, Data Science, and Robotics.

I am Sukundra Pujara, a Computer Science undergraduate from Nepal, currently pursuing a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence at Softwarica College of IT & E-Commerce, in collaboration with Coventry University. My academic and practical focus lies at the intersection of web development, artificial intelligence, data science, and robotics, with a commitment to building scalable software that addresses real-world challenges.

I have developed proficiency in Python, Django, JavaScript, React.js, and MySQL, and I take a full-stack approach to application development. This includes designing responsive user interfaces, building reliable backend services and APIs, and structuring databases for performance and maintainability. I am also broadening my skills in application development, with an emphasis on user-centered design, efficient data handling, and long-term scalability.

In artificial intelligence, I am building a solid foundation in machine learning and deep learning, covering supervised and unsupervised learning, data preprocessing, model training and evaluation, and neural network concepts. Through data science, I apply analytical and visualization techniques to transform raw data into meaningful insights. My interest in robotics reflects my wider ambition to work with intelligent systems that combine software, sensing, and decision-making in the physical world.

I learn through hands-on experimentation, practical project work, and the systematic breakdown of complex problems. I value clean code, logical reasoning, and continuous improvement. My objective is to contribute to impactful technology projects while advancing my career as a software developer and intelligent systems engineer. I welcome opportunities for collaboration, internships, and professional development.

Driven by technology. Focused on innovation. Committed to continuous growth.
